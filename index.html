★ Our recommended plan: Business Hosting

Business Hosting — Rs 1,999/month (Rs 19,990/year; Rs 11,990 for the first year under the current Black Friday offer).

Why Business is the right pick for most customers:

  1. It fits a real growing business. Personal only allows 1 website, 5 GB storage and 5 email accounts, which a business outgrows fast. Business gives 5 websites, 25 GB SSD storage and 25 email accounts — enough room to run a company site, a second project or a client site, and a proper email address for each team member, without paying for capacity most businesses will never use.
  2. Better protection for the money. Daily backups are kept for 3 days on Business instead of 1 day on Personal, so a mistake spotted a day or two late can still be undone. It also adds priority setup, managed DNS help and business email support.
  3. Professional is more than most need. Professional (Rs 3,999/month) is the right plan for a software house or corporate running up to 10 websites with 50 GB storage and 50 email accounts — but for a typical small or growing business it is double the price for headroom they will not use yet. They can upgrade later; PKHosting lets a plan grow with the customer.
  4. Best value per website. At the standard annual price, Business works out to about Rs 4,000 per website per year across its 5 websites, against Rs 6,990 for the single website on Personal.

Who should pick a different plan instead:

  • Personal (Rs 699/month) — the right choice for a student, hobbyist or individual with one small personal site or blog who wants the lowest cost and is happy with 5 GB storage and 1-day backup retention.
  • Professional (Rs 3,999/month) — the right choice for a software house, agency or corporate hosting several client or company sites, needing 10 websites, 50 GB storage, 5-day backup retention and priority support.

Bottom line: start on Business if you are running a business. Start on Personal if it is one personal site. Move to Professional when you are managing many sites and need the extra storage, backup cover and priority support.
